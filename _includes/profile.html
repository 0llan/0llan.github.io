About

Research through making.

I work at the intersection of human–computer interaction, haptics, and robotics, with a particular interest in experiences that connect physical sensation to virtual environments.

At the Human-Centered Intelligent Systems (HCIS) Lab of Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST), advised by Professor SeungJun Kim, I have contributed to research on biomechanical electrical muscle stimulation, ear-pressure feedback, magnetic haptics, wearable inertia rendering, and human–drone interaction.
